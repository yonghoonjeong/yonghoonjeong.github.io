Privacy & Analytics

Updated October 2026

This academic website is maintained by Yonghoon Jeong. Optional Google Analytics helps me understand which pages and research papers visitors find useful.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you select “Accept analytics.” Declining does not affect access to any content. You can change your choice using “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page. Your preference is stored in this browser for six months. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics collection and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies; it does not erase data already collected.

What is measured

With your permission, Google Analytics collects page views, approximate location, device and browser information, referral sources, scrolling, outbound link clicks, and CV and paper link clicks. This website does not send your name, email address, or contact-form information to Analytics, and it does not use Google signals or advertising personalization.

Google processes analytics data on its infrastructure. Learn how Google uses information from websites that use its services and review Google's Privacy Policy.

Who can see the reports

Analytics reports are available to the website owner through a private Google Analytics account. Visitor statistics are not published on this website.

Hosting and external links

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information to operate and secure its hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links, including paper, CV-hosting, and professional profile services, follow their respective providers' privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact yhjeong@navy.ac.kr.
