Meeting with the President of ETRI

Prof. Saewoong Bahk
Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI)

August 2026

During a research trip in August 2026, I visited Republic of Korea Navy Headquarters in Gyeryong for a look around, then returned to Seoul National University for the first time in six months to meet NETLAB members and conduct a brief USRP test. I also visited ETRI, where it was a pleasure to see my former advisor and mentor, Prof. Saewoong Bahk, again after some time.
