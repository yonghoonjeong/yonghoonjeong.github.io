Meeting with the Superintendent

Vice Admiral Park Gyu-baek
Republic of Korea Naval Academy

June 2026

Although his three-star rank initially felt intimidating, I approached the meeting as a faculty member meeting the head of the Academy. He recalled a philosophy course taught by a naval officer who had served in the same teaching role I now hold as the most meaningful class of his cadet years. I left hoping that my own students might one day remember my teaching with similar warmth. It was a deeply meaningful conversation.
