Teaching Evaluation Passed

Republic of Korea Naval Academy

August 2026

In August 2026, I was pleased to pass a teaching evaluation before faculty members in the Academy's science division. It was a valuable new experience: while my delivery was well received, the feedback highlighted the need to make the material more accessible. I will use these insights to better calibrate the difficulty of my lectures and support students' understanding.
