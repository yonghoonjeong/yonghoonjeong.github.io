Coastal Training with the 82nd Class

Republic of Korea Naval Academy

June–July 2026

During ten days embarked with the 82nd class, I visited Ulleungdo, Dokdo, Donghae, Pohang, and Busan. Setting foot on Dokdo and landing on Ulleungdo from an LST-II in the manner of a Marine Corps amphibious landing were rare and memorable experiences. The professionalism and dedication of the cadet training officers offered a glimpse of the officers these midshipmen might become. Above all, our many conversations gave me a valuable insight into cadet life.
